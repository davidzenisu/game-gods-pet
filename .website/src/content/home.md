# God's Pet

## A Unique Blend of Arcade Action and Social Deduction

God's Pet is an innovative multiplayer game that combines fast-paced arcade gameplay with psychological social deduction. Up to 4 players compete in a battle of wits, reflexes, and deception.

## How It Works

### The Animals
Players on PC take on the role of quirky animals competing in arcade-style challenges. Using split-screen gameplay, you'll dash, jump, and strategize your way through levels—all while trying to collect as many points as possible. But there's a twist: one of the other animals has a hidden advantage.

### The Gods
Meanwhile, players on their phones become Gods, each secretly assigned one of the animals as their "personal pet." Throughout the game, you'll strategically grant bonuses and blessings to your chosen animal, helping them rack up points. But beware—the other animals are watching carefully.

## The Challenge

As a God, your goal is straightforward: help your pet win without getting caught. Grant them power-ups, open secret passages for them while blocking others, or smite their enemies at the very last second. The catch? If you're too obvious, the other animals will figure out who your pet is by the end of the game. Once they do, their votes could expose you or save your pet from suspicion.

As an Animal, you're on high alert. Pay attention to who's getting suspiciously lucky. Which animal keeps getting blessed at just the right moment? Was that a coincidence, or is someone playing favorites? After the final round, cast your vote and try to unmask the Gods and their Pets.

## The Verdict

Only the sharpest players will win. Will you pull off the perfect heist as a God, or will you uncover the truth as an Animal? With every blessing granted and every suspicious pattern, the line between luck and favoritism grows thinner.

**God's Pet**: Where arcade action meets ultimate deception.
